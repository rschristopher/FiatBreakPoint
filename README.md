# FiatBreakPoint
Tracking the Cantillon Effect and the structural limits of the fiat monetary system


## Brainstorming

like FedWatch but focused on measurable and publicly available data of when the Fed (and other central banks?) will be forced to intervene.
Not just a prediction of what the Fed might do, but a measure of what they will be forced to do.

Perhaps track across three vectors (stack ranked by risk at any given time, with historical view as well)


1. Banking Sector Insolvency

aka CRE Debt Maturity Wall or The Refinancing Wall or Unrealized Balance Sheet Losses

track regional banks (holding trillions in low-interest property loans), and defaults, and predict force Fed intervention


2. Interbank Repo Market Lockup

aka Repo Market Funding Squeeze or Liquidity Crunch

the internal machinery where banks swap US Treasuries overnight for cash begins to dry up due to the fed draining liquidity via QT


3. Employment Trap Door

aka The Sahm Rule Trigger or The Non-Linear Unemployment Spike

unemployment operates on a psychological trigger, once it rises past a baseline threshold, it signals a locked-in recession (collapsed consumer demand)
